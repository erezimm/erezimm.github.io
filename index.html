Observational Astrophysicist  |  Supernovae  |  Time-Domain Astronomy

I am an observational astronomer studying supernovae of all kinds, from the first hours after explosion to late phases, to constrain their progenitor stars, map their mass-loss history, and understand their explosion mechanisms. I led the Nature study of the decadal supernova 2023ixf, for which I obtained HST UV spectra, and the study of the extremely faint Iax supernova 2024vjm from a pre-explosion Euclid image (in review, Nature Astronomy). I am PI of programs on the Hubble Space Telescope, Chandra, the VLT, and Gemini, and I developed CAST, the transient marshal of the LAST collaboration. PhD candidate at the Weizmann Institute of Science, expected Oct. 2026.

Collaborations

I am an active participant in major transient collaborations including LAST, BlackGEM, ePESSTO+, SoXS, ULTRASAT, and formerly ZTF, and a fellow of the Weizmann–Helmholtz Multi-Messenger School. I developed CAST, a TOM-based transient marshal for the LAST collaboration.
